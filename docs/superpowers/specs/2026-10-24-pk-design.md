Projects 9a and 9b of the "เพิ่มลูกเล่นการโดเนท" roadmap (master plan `C:\Users\ggtan\.claude\plans\1-2-drifting-lemur.md`, approved 2026-10-23).

- **Founder decision:** "เอาทั้งคู่เลย", i.e. build both versions.
- **Rulings from the plan:**
  - R3: only one PK of either kind at a time.
  - R6: one OBS link, `/pk`.
- **This document:** the shared model, plus the P9a details. P9b adds invitations on top of the same model (its own section below, built after P9a).
- Calls marked *ruling* are mine.

## Shared model

`PkMatch`:

| Field | Values / notes |
|---|---|
| `id` | |
| `kind` | `"solo"` (P9a) or `"duo"` (P9b) |
| `ownerId` | FK User, cascade |
| `opponentId?` | P9b only. FK User, `SetNull` |
| `status` | `PENDING` (P9b invite), `ACTIVE`, `ENDED`, `CANCELLED`, `DECLINED` |
| `nameA`, `nameB` | ≤ 20 chars |
| `colorA`, `colorB` | hex |
| `durationSec` | 300, 600 or 900 |
| `startedAt?`, `endsAt?`, `endedAt?` | |
| `totalA?`, `totalB?` | Int, frozen when the match ends |
| `createdAt` | |

Tip fields: `pkMatchId String?` (no FK, indexed) and `pkSide String?` (`"A"` | `"B"`).

**What counts**
- A tip counts when it was **sent during the match** (`createdAt` in `[startedAt, endsAt]`), carries that match and side, and is **CONFIRMED**.
- *Ruling P1:* counting by send time, not confirm time, means creators who confirm by hand don't lose tips.
- Late confirmations keep updating the live totals until the result is frozen:
  - The result freezes the first time anything reads the match after `endsAt + 60 s` (*ruling P2*: a grace minute for confirms in flight).
  - At that point `totalA`/`totalB` are stored and `status` becomes ENDED. Rejects and confirms after that don't change history.
- Totals are a SUM query over confirmed tips, so there is no per-tip pipeline hook. Reject drops a tip out automatically.

**Rules**
- **One at a time (R3):** under `lockCreator`, a creator can't start or accept a match while any PENDING or ACTIVE match lists them as owner or opponent.
- **No cron:** expiry is computed on read.

## P9a: two sides in one stream

**Creator**
- **Settings page** (`/dashboard/features/pk`):
  - **Start form:** side names (default ทีมแดง / ทีมน้ำเงิน), colours (default red `#ef4444` / blue `#3b82f6`), and duration chips 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
  - **Live panel:** totals, a countdown, and "จบก่อนเวลา" (sets `endsAt = now`, which freezes after the grace).
  - **History:** the last 10 matches with their final totals and winner.
- **Hub card** "PK" (`pk`):
  - icon `trophy`;
  - fanPage `true`;
  - `obsPath "/pk"`, `obsSize "800×220"`;
  - toggle `pkToggle`.
  - The card "on" only enables the feature. Matches are started from the page. Turning the card off cancels an ACTIVE solo match.

**Fan (donate page, only while a solo match is ACTIVE)**
- A "ทิปนี้เข้าฝั่ง" block with two big buttons in the side colours, showing the live totals and time left.
- Choosing a side is optional. "ไม่เลือก" means the tip doesn't count for either side.
- The server stores `pkMatchId`/`pkSide` only if that match is still ACTIVE and not past `endsAt`. Otherwise it is silently ignored; the tip itself is never affected.

**Overlay (`/overlay/<u>/pk`)**
- Polls every 2 s while there is a match, and every 5 s while idle.
- Shows a split bar in the two colours (proportional, 50/50 at 0–0), the names, the totals and an mm:ss countdown.
- At the end it shows a winner banner ("<name> ชนะ!" or "เสมอ!") with the final totals for 15 s after freeze, then hides.
- Hidden when there is no match.
- Test: a Redis test match (fake totals animating for 20 s), like the other tests.

**Feed:** `GET /api/overlay/<u>/pk?key=` returns `{match: {kind, nameA, nameB, colorA, colorB, totalA, totalB, endsAt, status, winner?} | null}`, with the test first.

## P9b: creator vs creator (built after P9a)

- **Invite:** creator A invites `@username` (another TipTang creator with the PK card on), with a duration of 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
  - This creates a PENDING duo match: `ownerId` = A, `opponentId` = B, names = the two display names, colours = each creator's `alertColor` or the defaults.
  - The invite expires after 10 minutes (on read).
- **B's side:**
  - B sees the invite on their PK page with accept or decline.
  - Accepting needs R3 for both creators, under both creators' locks taken in id order. On accept, both start together: `startedAt = now`.
  - Either side can cancel before or during the match. Cancelling during the match freezes the result as "ยกเลิก", with no winner.
- **Totals:** each side's total is that creator's own confirmed tips sent during the window. There is no fan side choice; every tip to that creator counts.
- **Overlay:** the same `/pk` overlay on both streams. Each stream sees its own creator on the left.
- **Privacy:** only display names, avatars and totals are shared between creators. No fan names, messages or money move.
- **Abuse:** no invite spam. At most 3 pending outgoing invites. A creator can turn on "ไม่รับคำเชิญ PK" (the default is to accept invites).

## Rulings

- **P1:** count by send time while the match runs.
- **P2:** results freeze at the end plus a 60 s grace, computed on read.
- **P3:** a fan's side choice is ignored silently when the match is over or not live.
- **P4:** turning the PK card off cancels a running solo match, which gets no result.
- **P5:** a duo match is cancelled when either creator turns the card off.
