# PK creator vs creator (P9b) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** A creator invites another TipTang creator to a timed PK. When the invite is accepted, both streams show the same race, and each side is that creator's own confirmed tips sent during the window.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-pk-design.md`, the P9b section plus Q6–Q12. The schema is already in place from P9a (`kind "duo"`, `opponentId`, `pkInvitesOff`).

## Global Constraints

- **Copy and data:** same as P9a: th/en parity, no emoji, gender-neutral. Only display names and totals cross between creators (Q12).
- **Locking:** both creators are locked in ascending id order. Nothing from PK runs inside a tip transaction.
- **Errors:** generic error replies, so nobody can probe who has PK on or invites off (Q7).

## Review Focus

1. Accept checks R3 for both creators under both locks. Two creators accepting crossing invites at the same time can't both end up in two matches.
2. Invite rules:
   - no self-invite;
   - at most 3 pending outgoing;
   - 1 h after a decline;
   - expires after 10 minutes;
   - the opponent's "ไม่รับคำเชิญ PK" is respected.
3. Each stream sees its own creator on the left (`me`/`them`). Totals count each creator's own confirmed tips in the window (DB clock).
4. Either side can cancel. Cancelling during the match shows no winner, and the card going off or the account being deleted cancels it (P5, Q8).
5. The grace for the freeze is the longer of the two creators' settings.
